A1 Module 1 — Everyday English Foundation

Grammar Foundation

1. Be Verb

I am, You are, He is, She is

I am a manager
She is my friend

2. Present Simple

Use the present simple for routines, habits and general facts

I work every day
He works in Yangon

3. Have / Has

Use have and has to talk about possession and relationships

I have two brothers
She has a car

4. Can

Use can for ability and simple requests

I can speak English
Can I have some water?

5. This / That

Use this and that for singular things

This is my phone
That is your bag

6. Some / Any

Useful for talking about quantities

I have some money
Do you have any questions?
