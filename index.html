Grammar Practice

Learn grammar through practical examples instead of memorizing rules only

Present Simple

Use for routines, habits, facts and regular activities

Pattern Subject + base verb
He/She/It + verb + s

Example: I work every day. She works in Yangon

Present Continuous

Use for actions happening now or around the present

Pattern Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

Example: I am studying English now

Past Simple

Use for completed actions in the past

Pattern Subject + past verb

Example: I visited Bagan last year

Present Perfect

Use for experiences, recent actions and unfinished time

Pattern Subject + have/has + past participle

Example: I have finished the report

Future

Use will and going to for predictions, decisions and plans

Pattern Subject + will + base verb

Example: We will discuss it tomorrow

Conditionals

Use conditional structures to discuss possibilities and consequences

Example If we improve service, customers may return
